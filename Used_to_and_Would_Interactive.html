The rules to take away
  • Used to + base form describes past habits and past states.
  • In questions: Did + subject + use to + base form?
  • In negatives: subject + didn’t use to + base form.
  • Would + base form describes repeated past actions when the past context is clear. We do not normally use it for past states.
