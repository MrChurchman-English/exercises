Overcoming the Creative Block

Another effective approach is to break large projects (5) into manageable chunks. Phrasal Verb Explanations & Target Types

  • 1. up (Particle): run up against — to encounter or experience a difficulty, problem, or opposition.
  • 2. put (Base Verb): put something down to — to attribute something to a particular cause.
  • 3. up (Particle): come up with — to think of or produce an idea, plan, or solution.
  • 4. sets (Base Verb): set in — (of something unpleasant) to begin and seem likely to continue.
  • 5. down (Particle): break down into — to divide or separate into smaller, more manageable parts.
  • 6. setting (Base Verb): set aside — to temporarily stop doing or thinking about something. (Requires -ing form after "try").
  • 7. up (Particle): crop up — to appear, happen, or come to light unexpectedly.
  • 8. give (Base Verb): give up on — to stop hoping or believing that something/someone will improve or succeed.
